# 🤖 LeadPilot AI

### AI-Powered Local Lead Generation & Sales Automation Platform

> **Discover. Qualify. Engage. Follow Up. Convert.**

LeadPilot AI is a multi-agent AI platform designed to help local businesses discover potential customers, qualify leads, handle customer interactions, schedule appointments, and automate sales follow-ups.

Instead of relying on a single AI assistant, LeadPilot AI uses **specialized AI sub-agents**, each responsible for a specific stage of the customer acquisition journey.

---

## 🚀 Overview

Local businesses often struggle with:

- Finding relevant prospects
- Identifying high-intent customers
- Responding to customer inquiries quickly
- Handling repetitive calls and questions
- Scheduling appointments
- Following up with leads consistently
- Converting qualified leads into meetings and customers

LeadPilot AI addresses these challenges by creating an **AI-powered digital sales team** that automates multiple stages of the lead-generation pipeline.

🔄 Customer Journey

LeadPilot AI follows a complete lead lifecycle:

1. Discover

2. Qualify

3. Engage

4. Capture

Collect relevant lead information.

5. Follow Up

Continue communication with qualified prospects.

6. Schedule

💡 Real-World Example

Imagine a dental clinic wants to acquire more customers.

Step 1 — Discovery

LeadPilot identifies relevant prospects and analyzes their business needs.

Step 2 — Engagement

A potential customer visits the clinic's website.

The AI Chatbot interacts with them.

Step 3 — Qualification

The AI determines whether the visitor represents a genuine business opportunity.

Step 4 — Phone Interaction

If the customer calls, the AI Receptionist can handle the inquiry and assist with appointment scheduling.

Step 5 — Follow-up

The AI Sales Agent follows up with qualified prospects.

Step 6 — Meeting

The prospect is moved toward a meeting or appointment.

Result

The business gets a more organized and automated lead-management workflow.

🔐 Responsible AI Considerations

Because LeadPilot AI works with customer and business interactions, a production implementation should include:

Secure handling of customer information
Authentication and authorization
Data encryption
Activity logging
Human approval for sensitive actions
Monitoring of AI-generated decisions
Configurable confidence thresholds
Protection against unauthorized access

AI-generated scores should be treated as decision-support signals, not guaranteed outcomes.

🚀 Getting Started
Clone the Repository
git clone https://github.com/YOUR-USERNAME/leadpilot-ai.git
cd leadpilot-ai
Install Dependencies
npm install
Configure Environment Variables

Create a .env file:

AI_API_KEY=your_api_key
DATABASE_URL=your_database_url

Never commit API keys or other secrets to GitHub.

Run the Application
npm run dev

Open the local development URL shown in your terminal.
